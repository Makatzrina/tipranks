08 — Questions

Things people ask before they sign up.

Is Ledgerline a brokerage?

No. We're a research and analysis platform. You hold your positions wherever you already do — we connect read-only to give you a clearer picture of what you own.

How do you score the analysts?

Every published rating is timestamped and tracked. We measure the realised one-year return against the analyst's call (price target, direction, sector benchmark) and update the score after each market close.

Where does the data come from?

Fourteen primary sources: SEC EDGAR filings, exchange feeds (NYSE, Nasdaq, LSE, Euronext), public analyst notes, transcripts, and Form 4 / 13F filings. We cite every claim down to its source row.

Do you give buy & sell recommendations?

We synthesise consensus and surface evidence — but we never give advice. The verdict is a clear summary of what professionals are doing, not a recommendation for you.

What happens after the free trial?

Nothing automatic. We email you two days before it ends and ask whether you'd like to continue. No card up front, no surprise charges.
